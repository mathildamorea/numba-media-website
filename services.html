Our Services

Numba Media provides a range of professional media production services for businesses, organisations, events and individuals.

Video Production

We provide professional video production for corporate events, interviews, documentaries, promotional videos and other projects.

Photography

Our photography services include corporate events, portraits, professional headshots and special events.

Drone and Aerial Production

We provide aerial photography and videography for events, promotional content and selected production projects.

Live Production

We provide multi-camera production and live-streaming services for events, conferences and other programs.

Post-Production

Our post-production services include video editing, graphics and preparation of finished media content.
